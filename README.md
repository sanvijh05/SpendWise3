SpendWise – Smart Money Management
Track Your Money. Spend It Wisely.
SpendWise is a simple and user-friendly money management web application that helps users track their daily income and expenses, monitor their balance, and manage their monthly budget.
🎯 Project Objective
The main objective of SpendWise is to help users understand where their money goes by recording income and expenses in one place. It makes personal financial management easier and encourages better spending habits.
